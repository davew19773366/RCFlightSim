THE BICKLEY MFC RC FLIGHT SIM - CLUB EDITION 2.1.5 PREVIEW
Bickley Model Flying Club, Sutton at Hone, Kent
========================================================

Learn to fly radio-controlled models at our own field, with Sir Bob, our
illustrious chairman, as your guide: 13 lessons from first take-off to jet low
passes, seven models to unlock, realistic wind, thermals and physics, and free
flight whenever you like.

NEW IN 2.1.5 (club edition)
  - Radar: a small, see-through map in the top-right corner, in every lesson and
    in free flight. It's drawn as you see the field from the pilot box (the flying
    area at the top, N at the bottom) and shows the plane as a yellow arrow, the
    no-fly zones in red and a pale wedge for the way the view is looking.
    Turn it off in Options > VISIBILITY AIDS > RADAR
  - Figure of eight lesson: the plane leaves a breadcrumb trail (blue in left
    turns, orange in right turns), so you can watch your 8 build up
  - Figure of eight lesson: a FLIGHT PATH button on screen shows a guide 8 in the
    sky (off to start with; it turns green where you've flown it). Tap or click
    it, or press BACK / SHARE on a controller, or P on the keyboard
  - Figure of eight lesson: the progress now reads LEFT CIRCLE 40%  RIGHT CIRCLE DONE
  - After a crash: PRESS A / X (or TAP X on touch screens)

NEW IN 2.1.4 (club edition)
  - Touch screens: tap menu lines directly (tap < > on a setting to change it, tap
    above or below the options list to scroll); tap the sides of the hangar to
    choose a model
  - Touch screens: a big YES, SIR BOB! button (and START, LET'S GO!, OK) instead
    of asking for an X button you can't see
  - Touch screens: the MENU button no longer flicks the menu open and shut again
  - Touch screens: the throttle goes back to zero after a crash or reset
  - Touch screens: the sticks sit higher, with dead zones round them; if pulling
    the elevator right down still drops Safari out of full screen, lifting your
    finger puts it straight back. The "Add to Home Screen" message is gone
  - Every setting is now kept automatically (wind, view, trims, aircraft tuning
    too) - no SAVE SETTINGS needed. The rates switch still starts on LOW
  - Scenery: farmland all round, as it really is - the only trees are the wood
    to the north-west (now a no-fly zone too) and a small copse past the
    south-west corner; low scrub from the ditches everywhere else, gun club included
  - Keyboard: the throttle starts closed, so 1 arms straight away (it used to sit
    at half until you held S)
  - Mouse: click menu lines directly; a click that starts a flight no longer
    throws a hand-launched glider by accident
  - The first screens say PRESS A / X / ENTER (A on an Xbox pad)
  - The font licence is now also inside index.html itself, so the web page
    carries it wherever it's hosted (FONT-LICENCE.txt is still in every package)

NEW IN 2.1.3 (club edition)
  - Touch screens: the throttle knob now shows where the throttle really is (it was
    drawn in the middle, looking like half throttle, until first touched) - in
    Mode 1 and Mode 2

NEW IN 2.1.2 (club edition)
  - Gun club (east of the field, past the next crop field) is now a strict no-fly
    zone: clubhouse and stands, a red boundary line and posts; warning
    40 m before the edge, alarm inside; in lessons, flying into it ends the lesson
  - Circuits lesson moved into the flying area south of the field: near side about
    90 m from the pilot box, well away from the gun club
  - Lessons that start in the air now begin on the near side of the circuit (about
    90 m out, 30 m up, flying along it into wind) - an easier place to start
  - Touch screens: on those air starts the on-screen throttle is set to half power
    to match the motor (it used to cut the motor at once)

WHAT'S IN IT
  - The Bickley field as it really is: pilot box, pits and V fence, shelter,
    car park, windsock, the ditch and scrub round the edge, the flying area to the
    south - and the gun club to the east, a strict no-fly zone
  - Seven models: high-wing trainer, high-wing sports model, 2m electric glider,
    foam flying wing, warbird, 3D foamie and a 70mm EDF jet
  - Mode 2 or Mode 1 (Options > STICK MODE); low rates at every start
  - Visibility aids for spotting the model: auto zoom, target brackets, enlarging
    a far-away model, bright liveries, a zoom box, landing arrows, a radar
  - Hold a stick click (or G) to glance at the windsock
  - Buddy box: plug in a second controller; the instructor holds LB / L1 to take over
  - Works with a controller, keyboard and mouse, or touch screen sticks

RUNNING IT
  Windows:  unzip anywhere and double-click BickleyMFC_RCFlightSim.exe.
            Progress is saved in %APPDATA%\BickleyMFCRCFlightSim.
            If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, choose
            "More info" then "Run anyway" (the exe isn't code-signed).
  Browser:  open index.html (one self-contained file), or the club's web link.
            Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; Edge on Xbox Series X works too.
            Progress is saved in the browser.
  Phones and tablets: hold it sideways and touch the screen - on-screen sticks
            and buttons appear, with arrows showing what to do next.
            iPad / Android: tap FULLSCREEN (Android goes full screen by itself).
            iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen gives the most screen room.

CONTROLS (shown for MODE 2; Mode 1 swaps throttle and elevator) - also on the CONTROLS page
  Controller (Xbox / PlayStation / most USB pads):
    Left stick      throttle (up/down) and rudder (left/right)
    Right stick     elevator and aileron
    LB / L1         arm / safe the motor
    RB / R1         low / high rates
    B / Circle      back; flaps / crow brakes in flight
    X / Square      undercarriage (or info)
    A / Cross       select; hand launch
    Y / Triangle    view: wide / zoom / box
    D-pad           menus; elevator + aileron trim in flight
    LT / RT         rudder trim
    LT + RT         menu (reset is in here) - or the Menu button
    Stick click     hold to glance at the windsock
  Keyboard + mouse:
    W / S           throttle up / down (stays where you leave it)
    A / D           rudder
    Arrow keys      elevator and aileron; up/down/left/right in menus
    1 / 2           arm the motor / low-high rates
    Enter, Space    select; hand launch (a mouse click or screen tap too)
    Backspace       back; flaps in flight
    Q / E           undercarriage / view
    I J K L         trim (like the D-pad)
    Z / C           rudder trim
    Esc or Tab      menu (reset is in here)
    G (hold)        glance at the windsock
    F11             full screen
