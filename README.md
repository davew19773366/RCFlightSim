# The Bickley MFC RC Flight Sim

Learn to fly radio-controlled models at the Bickley Model Flying Club field, Sutton at Hone, Kent, with Sir Bob, our illustrious chairman, as your guide.

**Play in your browser:** https://davew19773366.github.io/RCFlightSim/

Works with a controller, a keyboard and mouse, or touch screen sticks on phones and tablets. See `README.txt` for the controls and what's new.
