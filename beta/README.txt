THE UNOFFICIAL BICKLEY MFC RC FLIGHT SIM - CLUB EDITION 2.4.0 BETA 3
Bickley Model Flying Club, Sutton at Hone, Kent
=====================================================================

An unofficial fan game: see the DISCLAIMER at the end.

Learn to fly radio-controlled models at our own field, with Sir Bob, our
illustrious chairman, as your guide: 13 lessons from first take-off to jet low
passes, seven models to unlock, realistic wind, thermals and physics, and free
flight whenever you like.

NEW IN 2.4.0  (BETA 3 - on the /beta/ page while it's tried out; the live
              game stays on 2.3.0 until then)
  - The field looks more like the real thing, from photos taken at the club:
    - The pits fence: the V is open at its point, with the white railing
      down the middle and a gap either side, as you walk out to fly. The red
      safety fence is its real size, just in front of it, and the pilots' box
      railing is its real length with a road cone at each end. Positions were
      measured off an overhead photo
    - The windsock is just inside the pits fence by the opening
    - The shelter has its two open bays, with brick patios and white garden
      chairs, and the Union Flag on the pole, blowing with the wind like the
      windsock. The oil drum barbecue is there too
    - Cars and vans park on the grass (no gravel), with the mesh fence panels
      along the field side of the car park
    - Round the edges: the portaloo and junk pile in the north-west corner,
      a few more trees, and pylons and glasshouses on the horizon. A deeper
      blue sky and livelier grass
  - 550 HELI (beta 2): a collective-pitch helicopter for free flight, in
    the hangar from the start. The throttle stick is the collective (-12 to
    +12 degrees, about +3 to hover, a little above half stick); ARM is the
    throttle hold - arm it with the collective right down and the head spools
    up over about 5 seconds. The sticks command roll and pitch rates through a
    flybarless controller, so it holds whatever attitude you leave it at -
    like the real thing, it won't level itself. Heading-hold tail on the
    rudder stick. It has ground effect in a low hover, translational lift as
    it picks up speed, drifts with the wind, sinks in its own downwash if you
    come straight down too fast, and autorotates: hit the throttle hold with
    the collective low and the air coming up keeps the head turning for a
    flare. Tip it over on the ground with the rotor turning and it's a blade
    strike. HEAD on the screen is the rotor speed. Out of range (700 m)
    its failsafe cuts the motor and sets the collective just below zero,
    so it comes down in autorotation
  - CHRISTMAS (beta 3): choose OPTIONS > SEASON > CHRISTMAS for the field
    under snow - white fields and grass with the hedges, furrows and trees
    showing through, snow on the shelter roof and the patios, a heavy pale
    sky, and snow falling and drifting with the wind (FPV too). A Christmas
    tree with coloured lights and a star between the shelter's patios,
    fairy lights along the shelter, a snowman out on the grass to the right
    of the pilots' box, and MERRY CHRISTMAS on the main menu. It's off
    until you choose it and lasts until you close the game - your save
    keeps AUTUMN or SUMMER, so older versions sharing the save never see
    it. The snow is only scenery: the ground, the physics and the lessons
    are exactly the same
  - FPV VIEW (free flight): PAUSED > FPV VIEW flies from the plane's nose,
    banking and climbing with it, with a wide view like FPV goggles - you
    see your own shadow on the grass. Lessons are always flown from the
    pilots' box. It stays on until you turn it off or close the game
  - The hangar now shows each model on the grass in front of the pilots'
    box, looking out over the field (beta 2: the new safety fence was in
    the way behind it)
  - The "DON'T FLY OVER THE PITS" area now matches the fence: everything
    behind the V, starting at the opening, about 11 m behind the pilots' box.
    The pilots' box and safety fence are outside it. The gun club, the wood
    and the car park are unchanged, and lessons work exactly as before
  - Saves: the same as 2.3.0, so the beta and the live game share your
    progress and settings both ways

NEW IN 2.3.0
  - iPhone screen fix: with portrait lock on, the game turned
    sideways came up short with a black strip, and ARM / FLAPS sat under the
    status bar and notch - worst from a Home Screen icon, where iOS leaves the
    status bar out of the screen size it reports. It now fills the screen and
    every button keeps clear of the status bar, notch and home bar, whichever
    way round it's turned
  - RC TRANSMITTER: fly with your own radio through a USB simulator dongle
    (the kind sold for PC flight simulators) or a radio that plugs in as a USB
    joystick. OPTIONS > RC TRANSMITTER... walks you through it:
    throttle up and down, then each stick to both sides. It works out which
    channel is which, how far each one goes and which way round it is, so it
    doesn't matter how your radio is set up. A switch for X (hand launch, OK,
    try again) is optional. Works in the Windows version, and in Chrome, Edge
    or Firefox on a computer (a browser only notices the dongle once a stick
    moves). Not on iPhone or iPad - Safari doesn't see them.
  - With the transmitter there's no L1 arming: your transmitter's own throttle
    cut switch is the safety, as at the field. The game's RATES & EXPO still
    apply on top - set them to HIGH / 100% if you'd rather use your radio's
    own. LEFT/RIGHT on the RC TRANSMITTER line turns it on or off
  - TOO FAR BEHIND THE PILOT LINE now only warns once you're behind the line
    and past the field's hedges. Flying behind the pilots over our own field
    (climbing out over the north end of 04/22, turning behind the box) is fine
    - the pits and car park still have their own warnings
  - STICK SETUP is now CONTROLLER STICKS: it's for game controllers only, it's
    hidden on touch screens (where it did nothing useful), and it no longer
    affects touch sticks, the keyboard or a transmitter
  - Saves: this version keeps its save under a new name and copies your
    progress over from the older one the first time. Older versions' saves are
    never touched, so going back to an older version (or trying a beta) can't
    lose anything

NEW IN 2.2.1
  - iPad, iPhone and Android with portrait (orientation) lock on: the page stays
    portrait however you hold the device, and a web page isn't allowed to turn
    itself, so you used to be stuck on TURN YOUR DEVICE SIDEWAYS. Now the game
    draws itself turned sideways whenever the screen is portrait - hold the
    device on its side and it's the right way up, sticks and buttons included.
    The first time, a panel explains it and shows where the lock switch is
    (Control Centre on iPad/iPhone, Quick Settings > Auto-rotate on Android).
    Upside down? The round-arrow button in the corner flips it, and remembers.
    With the lock off, nothing changes: turn the device and it lays itself out
    normally. Computers are never turned
  - Passing a lesson you'd already passed no longer announces its NEW
    AIRCRAFT again. (Failing a lesson you've passed before never loses the
    pass, the aircraft or the lessons it opened.)

NEW IN 2.2.0 (club edition)
  - Figure of eight lesson redesigned: the yellow 8 in the sky is now what you're
    scored on. Fly along it and it turns green; the next bit ahead lights up white
    to show the way. Mostly green (about 80%) is a pass, in either direction.
    Very tolerant: 20 m either side of the line and any height above 5 m. A full
    left and a full right circle still passes too. The guide is on by default
    (FLIGHT PATH turns it off), and the loops are rounder and easier to follow
  - Controls can't get stuck: whenever the screen changes, the app is switched
    away from, the device is rotated or the window loses focus, every stick, key,
    button and mouse button is let go. A press can't carry over into the next
    screen either
  - Mouse: letting go of the button anywhere (not just over the game) counts
  - Sound: now checked automatically on every release by actually listening to
    the game - it starts on your first tap, key or click, the motor is heard,
    and it comes back after you switch apps or press the Xbox Guide button
    (iPad, iPhone, Android, PC keyboard, Xbox and PlayStation pads). Also
    fixed a harmless but untidy error the sound library left when Safari
    wasn't ready yet
  - Every lesson now flown by a robot test pilot before each release - a steady
    pilot and a beginner (slow reactions, wobbly thumbs, wanders off line), in
    calm air, 8 mph and 14 mph gusts. The beginner gets three goes, like you do.
    What it found, and what changed:
  - Touch and go: stopping on the field is no longer the end - open the throttle
    and go again (you have 12 seconds)
  - Landings: where the wheels touch down counts. Landed on the field and rolled
    off the end into the crop? Still a landing
  - Hover: starts higher (45 m) to give you room to get it wrong, and a nose
    50 degrees up or more, slow, held for 3 seconds is a pass. In FORGIVING
    crashes the prop's torque and corkscrew are gentler on the 3D foamie
  - Warbird: the flaps used to balloon it up like a kite - now they're mostly
    drag, as they should be. The field counts for the warbird and jet landings
    (the runway's still best). FORGIVING: the tip stall is softer
  - FORGIVING crashes: a prop brushing the grass on a fast take-off or landing
    roll is no longer a prop strike, and straying into the gun club or the wood
    during a lesson gives you 2 seconds to turn back (was 1)
  - Behind the scenes: a full automatic test run on every release (PC, Xbox and
    PlayStation pads, iPad, iPhone, Android, both editions), then a beta site
    and a short real-device check before anything goes live

WHAT'S IN IT
  - The Bickley field as it really is: pilot box, pits and V fence, shelter,
    car park, windsock, the ditch and scrub round the edge, the flying area to the
    south - and the gun club to the east, a strict no-fly zone
  - Seven models: high-wing trainer, high-wing sports model, 2m electric glider,
    foam flying wing, warbird, 3D foamie and a 70mm EDF jet
  - Mode 2 or Mode 1 (Options > STICK MODE); low rates at every start
  - Visibility aids for spotting the model: auto zoom, target brackets, enlarging
    a far-away model, bright liveries, a zoom box, landing arrows, a radar and
    a sky compass
  - Hold a stick click (or G) to glance at the windsock
  - Buddy box: plug in a second controller; the instructor holds LB / L1 to take over
  - Works with a controller, keyboard and mouse, or touch screen sticks

RUNNING IT
  Windows:  unzip anywhere and double-click BickleyMFC_RCFlightSim.exe.
            Progress is saved in %APPDATA%\BickleyMFCRCFlightSim.
            If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, choose
            "More info" then "Run anyway" (the exe isn't code-signed).
  Browser:  open index.html (one self-contained file), or the club's web link.
            Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; Edge on Xbox Series X works too.
            Progress is saved in the browser.
  Phones and tablets: hold it sideways and touch the screen - on-screen sticks
            and buttons appear, with arrows showing what to do next.
            iPad / Android: tap FULLSCREEN (Android goes full screen by itself).
            iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen gives the most screen room.

CONTROLS (shown for MODE 2; Mode 1 swaps throttle and elevator) - also on the CONTROLS page
  Controller (Xbox / PlayStation / most USB pads):
    Left stick      throttle (up/down) and rudder (left/right)
    Right stick     elevator and aileron
    LB / L1         arm / safe the motor
    RB / R1         low / high rates
    B / Circle      back; flaps / crow brakes in flight
    X / Square      undercarriage (or info)
    A / Cross       select; hand launch
    Y / Triangle    view: wide / zoom / box
    D-pad           menus; elevator + aileron trim in flight
    LT / RT         rudder trim
    LT + RT         menu (reset is in here) - or the Menu button
    Stick click     hold to glance at the windsock
  Keyboard + mouse:
    W / S           throttle up / down (stays where you leave it)
    A / D           rudder
    Arrow keys      elevator and aileron; up/down/left/right in menus
    1 / 2           arm the motor / low-high rates
    Enter, Space    select; hand launch (a mouse click or screen tap too)
    Backspace       back; flaps in flight
    Q / E           undercarriage / view
    I J K L         trim (like the D-pad)
    Z / C           rudder trim
    Esc or Tab      menu (reset is in here)
    G (hold)        glance at the windsock
    F11             full screen
