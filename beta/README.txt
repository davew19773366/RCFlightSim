THE UNOFFICIAL BICKLEY MFC RC FLIGHT SIM - CLUB EDITION 2.2.0 BETA
Bickley Model Flying Club, Sutton at Hone, Kent
=====================================================================

An unofficial fan game: see the DISCLAIMER at the end.

Learn to fly radio-controlled models at our own field, with Sir Bob, our
illustrious chairman, as your guide: 13 lessons from first take-off to jet low
passes, seven models to unlock, realistic wind, thermals and physics, and free
flight whenever you like.

NEW IN 2.2.0 (club edition)  (BETA - being checked on real devices before release)
  - Figure of eight lesson redesigned: the yellow 8 in the sky is now what you're
    scored on. Fly along it and it turns green; the next bit ahead lights up white
    to show the way. Mostly green (about 80%) is a pass, in either direction.
    Very tolerant: 20 m either side of the line and any height above 5 m. A full
    left and a full right circle still passes too. The guide is on by default
    (FLIGHT PATH turns it off), and the loops are rounder and easier to follow
  - Controls can't get stuck: whenever the screen changes, the app is switched
    away from, the device is rotated or the window loses focus, every stick, key,
    button and mouse button is let go. A press can't carry over into the next
    screen either
  - Mouse: letting go of the button anywhere (not just over the game) counts
  - Every lesson now flown by a robot test pilot before each release - a steady
    pilot and a beginner (slow reactions, wobbly thumbs, wanders off line), in
    calm air, 8 mph and 14 mph gusts. The beginner gets three goes, like you do.
    What it found, and what changed:
  - Touch and go: stopping on the field is no longer the end - open the throttle
    and go again (you have 12 seconds)
  - Landings: where the wheels touch down counts. Landed on the field and rolled
    off the end into the crop? Still a landing
  - Hover: starts higher (45 m) to give you room to get it wrong, and a nose
    50 degrees up or more, slow, held for 3 seconds is a pass. In FORGIVING
    crashes the prop's torque and corkscrew are gentler on the 3D foamie
  - Warbird: the flaps used to balloon it up like a kite - now they're mostly
    drag, as they should be. The field counts for the warbird and jet landings
    (the runway's still best). FORGIVING: the tip stall is softer
  - FORGIVING crashes: a prop brushing the grass on a fast take-off or landing
    roll is no longer a prop strike, and straying into the gun club or the wood
    during a lesson gives you 2 seconds to turn back (was 1)
  - Behind the scenes: a full automatic test run on every release (PC, Xbox and
    PlayStation pads, iPad, iPhone, Android, both editions), then a beta site
    and a short real-device check before anything goes live

WHAT'S IN IT
  - The Bickley field as it really is: pilot box, pits and V fence, shelter,
    car park, windsock, the ditch and scrub round the edge, the flying area to the
    south - and the gun club to the east, a strict no-fly zone
  - Seven models: high-wing trainer, high-wing sports model, 2m electric glider,
    foam flying wing, warbird, 3D foamie and a 70mm EDF jet
  - Mode 2 or Mode 1 (Options > STICK MODE); low rates at every start
  - Visibility aids for spotting the model: auto zoom, target brackets, enlarging
    a far-away model, bright liveries, a zoom box, landing arrows, a radar and
    a sky compass
  - Hold a stick click (or G) to glance at the windsock
  - Buddy box: plug in a second controller; the instructor holds LB / L1 to take over
  - Works with a controller, keyboard and mouse, or touch screen sticks

RUNNING IT
  Windows:  unzip anywhere and double-click BickleyMFC_RCFlightSim.exe.
            Progress is saved in %APPDATA%\BickleyMFCRCFlightSim.
            If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, choose
            "More info" then "Run anyway" (the exe isn't code-signed).
  Browser:  open index.html (one self-contained file), or the club's web link.
            Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; Edge on Xbox Series X works too.
            Progress is saved in the browser.
  Phones and tablets: hold it sideways and touch the screen - on-screen sticks
            and buttons appear, with arrows showing what to do next.
            iPad / Android: tap FULLSCREEN (Android goes full screen by itself).
            iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen gives the most screen room.

CONTROLS (shown for MODE 2; Mode 1 swaps throttle and elevator) - also on the CONTROLS page
  Controller (Xbox / PlayStation / most USB pads):
    Left stick      throttle (up/down) and rudder (left/right)
    Right stick     elevator and aileron
    LB / L1         arm / safe the motor
    RB / R1         low / high rates
    B / Circle      back; flaps / crow brakes in flight
    X / Square      undercarriage (or info)
    A / Cross       select; hand launch
    Y / Triangle    view: wide / zoom / box
    D-pad           menus; elevator + aileron trim in flight
    LT / RT         rudder trim
    LT + RT         menu (reset is in here) - or the Menu button
    Stick click     hold to glance at the windsock
  Keyboard + mouse:
    W / S           throttle up / down (stays where you leave it)
    A / D           rudder
    Arrow keys      elevator and aileron; up/down/left/right in menus
    1 / 2           arm the motor / low-high rates
    Enter, Space    select; hand launch (a mouse click or screen tap too)
    Backspace       back; flaps in flight
    Q / E           undercarriage / view
    I J K L         trim (like the D-pad)
    Z / C           rudder trim
    Esc or Tab      menu (reset is in here)
    G (hold)        glance at the windsock
    F11             full screen
